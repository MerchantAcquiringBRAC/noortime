## Description (English)
NoorTime shows today's five prayer times, sunrise, and a live countdown to the next prayer.

- Prayer times for your city or your current location
- Bangladesh cities included; Karachi method and Hanafi Asr by default
- Choose Hanafi or Shafi Asr and other calculation methods
- Adjust each prayer by a few minutes to match your local mosque
- Reminders with a short sound for each prayer
- English and Bengali, light and dark themes
- No account, no ads, no tracking

Please note: times are calculated and may differ slightly from your local mosque timetable; use the minute adjustments to match it. Reminder sounds follow your iPhone's Silent switch, Focus and notification settings, and the sound is a short clip, not the full Adhan.

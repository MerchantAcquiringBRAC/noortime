# NoorTime Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE – fill in before publishing]_

NoorTime does not have a server or user accounts, and it does not collect, store or send your personal data to the developer.

## What stays on your phone
- Your chosen city or GPS place (GPS is rounded to about 1 km)
- Your settings: language, theme, calculation method, Asr school, minute adjustments, reminder choices

This data is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Location
- Location is requested only when you tap "Use my current location", and only while the app is open.
- NoorTime takes one position. It does not track you and does not run in the background.
- To show a city name, iOS itself may send the rounded coordinate to Apple's map service. This is done by Apple, not by NoorTime. See Apple's privacy policy.
- You can use the app without GPS by choosing a city.

## Notifications
Prayer reminders are local notifications created on your phone. No notification server is used.

## Analytics, ads, tracking
None. NoorTime contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

## Children
NoorTime collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
